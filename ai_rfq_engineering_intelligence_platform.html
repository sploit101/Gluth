Operational Requirements Summary

The requested production system is an automated line designed to process flexible hoses in 3 distinct coil diameters (8mm, 10mm, 12mm) up to a length of 7,000mm . Machine scale requires integrated re-coiling buffers to comply with maximum floor footprint limits .

Internal hose lubrication mandates dual-grease dosage changeover between Grease Type 1 and Type 2 without cross-contamination . Software integration must strictly implement OPC-UA telemetry encryption per revised customer standard .

Open Questions & Risk Flags (⚑ 3 Found)
  • Unspecified Cap Torque: Customer specification does not specify maximum tightening torque for hose end screw caps .
  • Software Standard Delta: Bosch Norm N2580 updated to v4.0 (MQTT broker required; standard 2023 PLC template is non-compliant).
  • Cycle Time Ambiguity: Target takt time stated as "under 18s" but does not define coil changeover overhead.

CONFIDENTIAL // BMW GROUP SPECIFICATION
SECTION 3.2 — HOSE FEEDING, CUTTING & GREASING PARAMS

Document Ref: BMW-GS-95024-3 | Version 2.8 | Author: Engineering Standard Dept.

[REF 3.2.1] The hose processing machine shall accept flexible hose stock from three (3) parallel decoiler positions handling 8mm, 10mm, and 12mm internal diameter hoses up to a maximum uncoiled cut length of 7,000mm.
[REF 3.2.4] To satisfy the physical footprint restriction of Assembly Cell Bay #4, cut hose sections exceeding 3,000mm must undergo immediate inline re-coiling prior to station transfer.
[REF 4.1.2] Internal lubrication unit must support dynamic changeover between Grease Grade 1 (Standard synthetic) and Grease Grade 2 (High temp) with automatic purge cycle.
[REF 8.0.1] PLC Software interface mandatory telemetry protocol updated to Bosch N2580-10 v4.0 requiring encrypted OPC-UA endpoints over TLS 1.3.
[REF 9.3.0] End cap sealing screw assembly step required for variant B (non-fitted variant). Cap torque parameters to be agreed upon during pre-acceptance testing.
